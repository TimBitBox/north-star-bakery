About our neighborhood bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make the neighborhood feel more connected through fresh bread. Our counter welcomes early commuters, families, and neighbors planning a special occasion.

How we choose our ingredients

We focus on dependable flour, seasonal flavors, and small batches. We seek nearby suppliers when practical and choose ingredients for their flavor and consistency.
